# Abhijith Padukana

**Biotechnology Researcher · Nanobiotechnology · Mycology · Scientific Instrumentation · Embedded Systems**

I am a Research Scholar at the **Nanobiotechnology Laboratory, Department of Studies in Biotechnology, University of Mysore, Mysuru, Karnataka, India**. My work sits at the intersection of experimental biology and engineering: microbial and fungal systems, nanobiotechnology, biological data management, scientific instrumentation, embedded control, and field-oriented digital tools.

## Current open work

### MycoTRACE

A self-hosted mushroom specimen, culture, and research-data system built around permanent specimen identity, traceable derivatives, standardized imaging, QR-linked records, offline field capture, controlled vocabularies, publication snapshots, and reproducible data export.

Repository: **[MycoTRACE](https://github.com/abhijith-padukana/MycoTRACE)**

### ESP8266 Micro Weather Monitoring System

A preserved 2017 ESP8266 + DHT11 proof-of-concept, maintained as a documented example of early embedded environmental monitoring work.

Repository: **[micro_weather_monitoring_system](https://github.com/abhijith-padukana/micro_weather_monitoring_system)**

### Embedded systems archive index

Selected historical Arduino and embedded-system projects are reviewed for provenance, safety, documentation, and reproducibility before being promoted from the private engineering archive.

Repository: **[Arduino-Projects](https://github.com/abhijith-padukana/Arduino-Projects)**

## Repository philosophy

I use GitHub as a curated engineering and research-development record. Legacy experiments remain archived privately until their provenance, credentials, third-party material, hardware assumptions, and documentation have been reviewed. Public repositories are intended to be understandable, reproducible, and explicit about their validation status.
